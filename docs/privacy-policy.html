NeuroStudy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

Short version: NeuroStudy does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All study progress is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Overview

NeuroStudy ("we," "our," or "the app") is an educational application for studying neuroanatomy and neuropsychology. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information We Do Not Collect

NeuroStudy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Health or medical information
  • Crash logs sent to third-party services

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

NeuroStudy stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults system:

  • Your quiz progress and mastery levels for each brain structure
  • App preferences (dark mode, reduced motion, haptic feedback, quiz length)
  • Spaced repetition scheduling data

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app.

Third-Party Services

NeuroStudy does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social networking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

Children's Privacy

NeuroStudy is intended for students aged 17 and older (college/university level). The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Educational Disclaimer

All content within NeuroStudy — including neuroanatomical descriptions, DSM-5 diagnostic criteria summaries, and clinical information — is provided for educational purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for clinical decisions.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our support page.
